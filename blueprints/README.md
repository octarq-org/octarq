## What is an Octarq Blueprint?

Octarq is a self-hosted single-binary operations backend providing short links, custom-domain email, and DNS automation out of the box. 

An **Octarq Blueprint** is an end-to-end operational recipe that combines:
1. **Agent Prompts & Workflows**: Pre-engineered SOPs for AI assistants (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex).
2. **MCP Integration Templates**: Ready-to-use configuration for stdio and remote HTTP/SSE Model Context Protocol connections.
3. **Execution Scripts & Recipes**: Automated tasks for business monitoring, lead acquisition, and transactional email verification.
