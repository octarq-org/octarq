The Octarq Mailboxes engine processes inbound transactional emails, provisions on-the-fly Catch-All mailboxes, renders raw EML files with attachment inspection, and dispatches outbound mail via encrypted SMTP senders.

## 1. Step-by-Step Console Workflows

### 1. Connecting Inbound Email via Cloudflare Worker Webhook
1. Go to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Workspace**, and copy your workspace **Inbound Webhook URL** (`/api/webhook/{orgSlug}/email/inbound/{token}`).
2. In your Cloudflare Dashboard under **Email Routing $\rightarrow$ Email Workers**, deploy a worker script to POST raw inbound emails to this endpoint.
3. Once deployed, emails sent to your custom domain appear live in the Octarq Mail console.

### 2. Enabling Catch-All & Reserved Prefixes
1. Go to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Workspace**.
2. Toggle ON **Enable Catch-All Routing**.
3. Under **Reserved Mailbox Prefixes**, enter comma-separated prefixes (e.g. `admin, support, postmaster`) to prevent Catch-All from capturing reserved system addresses.

### 3. Adding Outbound SMTP Senders
1. Navigate to **Operations $\rightarrow$ Mailboxes**, and switch to the **SMTP Senders** tab.
2. Click **Add SMTP Sender**.
3. Enter your SMTP Host (e.g. `smtp.mailgun.org`), Port (`587`), Username, Password, and default From Email (`noreply@example.com`).
4. Click **Save**. Password credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-GCM and never returned via API endpoints.

---

## 2. Important Operating Cautions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Outbound Transactional Mail**: System-generated emails (member invitations, password resets, verification emails) automatically use the first configured SMTP sender in your workspace.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Why does an incoming email show "SPF / DMARC Failed"?
**Answer**: This indicates the sender's domain DNS lacks valid SPF/DMARC TXT records or was forwarded through an unauthenticated relay. Inspect the raw auth headers directly inside the email viewer.

### Q2: How can I receive instant alerts when outbound mail dispatch fails?
**Answer**: Go to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Notifications** in the console and configure Telegram or HTTP Webhooks to listen for email dispatch failures.
