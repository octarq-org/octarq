The Octarq Short Links module lets you manage branded short URLs, configure conditional dynamic targeting rules (Geo/Device/Language), enforce security thresholds, and automatically shorten outgoing email links.

## 1. Step-by-Step Console Workflows

### 1. Creating a Short Link
1. Go to **Operations → Marketing → Links** (or press `⌘K` and search `Create Link`).
2. Click **New Link**.
3. Enter your destination **Target URL** (e.g. `https://my-product.com/launch-deal`).
4. (Optional) Select a custom domain (e.g. `l.mybrand.com`) and custom slug (e.g. `launch`).
5. Click **Create**.

### 2. Setting Up Dynamic Targeting Rules
During creation or editing, click **Add Rule** to set conditional redirects:
- **Geo Matching (GeoIP)**: Route visitors by country code (e.g. `US` visitors -> US landing page).
- **Device & OS Matching**: Route mobile visitors (iOS / Android) straight to app store downloads.
- **Browser Language**: Match preferred accept-language headers (e.g. `zh-CN` -> Chinese docs).

### 3. Access Controls & Limits
- **Password Protection**: Visitors must enter the passcode before being redirected.
- **Expiration & Click Limits**: Set an `ExpiresAt` date or total `ClickLimit`. When reached, visitors are routed to your **Expired URL** fallback.

## 2. Operating Cautions & Reserved Slugs

> [!CAUTION]
> **Protected Slugs**: Built-in system prefixes (`/admin`, `/api`, `/assets`, `/portal`, `/help`) are reserved by the console and cannot be used as short link custom slugs.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Why are some clicks reported without geographic location?
**Answer**: GeoIP resolution relies on public IP header forwarding (`X-Real-IP`, `X-Forwarded-For`). Clicks from localhost or unforwarded proxies are logged without GeoIP country codes.

### Q2: How do I enable auto-shortening for outgoing emails?
**Answer**: Navigate to **Operations → Messaging → Mail → Settings → Inbound Configuration** and check **Auto-shorten Links in Outgoing Mail**. External URLs in outbound transactional emails will be automatically wrapped.
