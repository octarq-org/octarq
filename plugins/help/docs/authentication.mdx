Octarq includes a comprehensive security and authentication engine that supports email/password credentials, two-factor authentication (TOTP 2FA), OAuth single sign-on (Google and GitHub), secure password recovery, registration email verification, and central session management.

## Key Capabilities

- **Multi-Factor Authentication (TOTP 2FA)**: Secure accounts with time-based one-time passwords (compatible with Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy) and bcrypt-hashed one-time recovery codes.
- **OAuth 2.0 Integration**: Enable one-click OAuth sign-in with Google and GitHub. OAuth users are automatically marked as email-verified.
- **Password Reset Flow**: Request password reset links via `/api/auth/forgot`. Secret tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes and expire in 1 hour. Resetting a password automatically revokes all active browser sessions.
- **Registration Email Verification**: Issue verification links (`/api/auth/verify-email`) on sign-up with optional instance-level login gating.
- **Instance-Level Sign-In Policies**:
  - **Public Registration (`allow_registration`)**: Toggle public sign-up on or off (default: ON).
  - **Require Email Verification (`require_email_verification`)**: Require users to verify their email address before signing in (default: OFF).
- **Session Management & Global Revocation**: Inspect active sessions (with client IP, location, user-agent) and execute "Sign out of all devices" to immediately invalidate all active cookies.

## How to Use

### 1. Setting Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
1. Go to **Settings → Account → Security** (or navigate to `/settings`).
2. Under **Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP)**, click **Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the displayed QR code with your authenticator app (or manually enter the setup key).
4. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code and click **Confirm & Enable**.
5. **Save your single-use recovery codes in a safe place.** If you lose access to your authenticator device, recovery codes are the only way to log in.

### 2. Resetting a Forgotten Password
1. On the Sign-In page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your account email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Click the link delivered to your email inbox (valid for 1 hour) to enter a new password.
4. Upon successful reset, all existing active sessions are terminated, and you are prompted to sign in with your new password.

### 3. Configuring OAuth Providers (Instance Admins)
1. Navigate to **Settings → Instance → Authentication**.
2. Expand the **Google** or **GitHub** provider section.
3. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret** (retrieved from your provider's developer console).
4. Register the exact **Callback URL** shown in Octarq (e.g., `https://your-octarq.com/api/auth/google/callback`).
5. Click **Save**. The OAuth login buttons will automatically appear on the Sign-In page.

### 4. Enforcement Policies (Instance Admins)
In **Settings → Instance → Authentication**:
- **Allow public sign-up**: Toggle to disable self-serve registration, making Octarq invite-only.
- **Require Email Verification**: Toggle to block logins for unverified email accounts until they complete the email verification link.

:::note
OAuth and OIDC users (Google, GitHub, or Enterprise SSO) are verified by the identity provider and are automatically treated as email-verified without receiving verification emails.
:::

## Why It Matters

A robust authentication system protects your single-person or multi-user instance against credential stuffing, unauthorized account creation, and session hijacking, while providing frictionless OAuth sign-in options for your team.
