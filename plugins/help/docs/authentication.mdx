Octarq supports email and password authentication, TOTP two-factor authentication (2FA), Google and GitHub OAuth sign-in, password reset, and session management.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Security**.
2. Under **Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP)**, click **Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the displayed QR code with your authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, 1Password, or Authy).
4. Enter the 6-digit code from your app and click **Confirm & Enable**.
5. Save your single-use recovery codes in a secure location.

> [!CAUTION]
> If you lose your authenticator device, recovery codes are the only way to log back into your account.

## Password Reset

1. Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your account email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the link sent to your email (valid for 1 hour) and set a new password.
4. Setting a new password automatically logs out all active browser sessions across all devices.

## OAuth Provider Setup (Instance Admins)

1. Go to **Settings → Instance → Authentication**.
2. Expand the **Google** or **GitHub** section.
3. Enter the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** obtained from the provider's developer console.
4. Add the callback URL shown in Octarq (`https://your-octarq-domain.com/auth/callback/google`) to your provider settings.
5. Click **Save**. The OAuth login buttons will appear on the sign-in page.

## Registration and Sign-In Policies (Instance Admins)

Go to **Settings → Instance → Authentication** to adjust registration settings:

- **Allow public sign-up**: Turn off to disable self-serve registration and make the instance invite-only.
- **Require Email Verification**: Turn on to block unverified email accounts from signing in until they complete the email verification link.

> [!NOTE]
> Accounts created or authenticated via OAuth (Google or GitHub) are automatically marked as email-verified.
