Octarq isolates resources into independent organizations (workspaces). Each workspace maintains its own domains, mailboxes, short links, API tokens, and member permissions.

## Role Permissions

- **Owner**: Full management privileges, including billing, workspace deletion, member role assignment, and owner transfer.
- **Admin**: Configures workspace settings, domain zones, mailboxes, short links, and member invitations.
- **Member**: Accesses operational features and views without access to administrative workspace settings.

## Switch Workspaces

1. Click the **Workspace Selector** at the top of the left sidebar.
2. Select any organization from the list to switch active workspaces.

## Create a New Workspace

1. Open the **Workspace Selector** at the top of the left sidebar.
2. Click **+ Create Workspace**.
3. Enter a workspace name (such as `Acme Corp`).
4. Click **Create & Switch**.

## Invite Team Members

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter the invitee's email address and assign a role (**Member**, **Admin**, or **Owner**).
4. Click **Send Invite**. An email containing an activation link valid for 24 hours will be sent.

## Modify Member Roles or Remove Access

Go to **Settings → Workspace → Members**:

- **Change Role**: Select a new role from the dropdown next to the member. Only an Owner can assign or remove the Owner role. The last remaining Owner cannot be demoted or removed until another member is promoted to Owner.
- **Remove Member**: Click **Remove** next to a member to revoke their access to the workspace immediately.
