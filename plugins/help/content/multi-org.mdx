Octarq is built on a multi-tenant architecture that allows users to create, manage, and switch between isolated **Organizations** (Workspaces). Each workspace maintains its own domains, mailboxes, short links, API tokens, and team members with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

## Key Capabilities

- **Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Data entities (links, mailboxes, DNS zones, webhooks, audit logs) are bound to a specific Organization ID (`OrgID`), ensuring total logical isolation between environments.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**:
  - **Owner**: Full administrative privileges over the workspace, including billing, workspace deletion, and member management.
  - **Admin**: Can manage domains, mailboxes, short links, settings, and team members.
  - **Member**: Access to operational tools and views without administrative workspace settings privileges.
- **Seamless Workspace Switching**: Switch between personal and team workspaces instantly via the sidebar switcher without logging out.
- **Email Invitation System**: Invite team members by email. System dispatches a secure 24-hour expiration link for password setup.
- **Instance Admin Privileges**: The initial operator account holds instance-wide administrative privileges (`IsInstanceAdmin`) to configure system settings across all organizations.

## How to Use

### 1. Switching Workspaces
1. Click the **Workspace Selector** at the top of the left sidebar rail.
2. Select any organization from your list to switch your active workspace session.

### 2. Creating a New Workspace
1. Open the **Workspace Selector** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Create Workspace**.
3. Enter your new workspace name (e.g., `Acme Corp` or `Client Project B`).
4. Click **Create & Switch**. A new organization is provisioned with a unique URL slug and inbound webhook token.

### 3. Inviting Team Members
1. Go to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Workspace $\rightarrow$ Workspace Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter the invitee's email address and assign their role (**Member**, **Admin**, or **Owner**).
4. Click **Send Invite**. The invitee receives an email with an activation link valid for 24 hours.

### 4. Revoking Member Access
Navigate to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Workspace $\rightarrow$ Workspace Members** and click **Remove** next to any member to revoke their access to the workspace immediately.

:::note
Deleting a workspace or revoking a member's access from one organization does not affect their membership or data in other organizations.
:::

## Why It Matters

Multi-tenant organization support allows freelancers, agencies, and growing startups to manage multiple client projects, separate staging and production environments, or collaborate with team members from a single Octarq deployment.
