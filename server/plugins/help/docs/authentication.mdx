Octarq provides account and identity controls to protect your workspaces and team members. You can sign in with email and password, enable two-factor authentication, reset passwords, connect OAuth providers, and manage registration policies.

## Sign In with Email and Password

Octarq protects your credentials and active sessions:

- **Password storage**: Passwords are encrypted at rest with unique salts. Octarq never logs or stores plaintext passwords.
- **Session management**: Sign-in sessions use secure browser cookies. Logging out invalidates your active session immediately.

## Set Up Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP)

Add a second layer of defense to your account using time-based one-time passwords:

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Security**.
2. Click **Enable 2FA** under **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan the displayed QR code with your authenticator app (such as 1Password, Google Authenticator, or Apple Keychain).
4. Enter the 6-digit verification code from your app and click **Confirm & Enable**.
5. Save your recovery codes in a secure password vault. Octarq provides 10 single-use emergency recovery codes.

:::caution Save Recovery Codes
Recovery codes are the only way to recover account access if you lose your authenticator device. Each code can only be used once.
:::

## Reset Your Password

Recover your account password through email verification:

1. Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your account email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email from Octarq and click the reset link within 1 hour.
4. Enter a new password and confirm.
5. Sign in with your new password. Octarq automatically ends all other active browser sessions for your account.

## Create an Account

Create a personal workspace with an email address and a password:

1. Click **Create one** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your email address, a password, and a workspace name.
3. Click **Create account**.
4. Open the email from Octarq and click the link within 24 hours.
5. Sign in with your email address and password.

Octarq sends one message to the address in every case. If the address already has an account, the message tells you to sign in. The sign-in screen does not say which case it is, so that no one can use the sign-up page to find out who has an account.

## Configure OAuth Providers

Instance administrators can enable Google and GitHub sign-in for team members:

1. Go to **Settings → Instance → Authentication**.
2. Expand the **Google** or **GitHub** section.
3. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret** (credentials are encrypted at rest).
4. Add the callback URL to your OAuth application settings:
   - Google: `https://your-octarq-host.com/auth/callback/google`
   - GitHub: `https://your-octarq-host.com/auth/callback/github`
5. Click **Save**. The sign-in button appears on your login page immediately. Accounts authenticated through OAuth are verified automatically.

## Manage Registration Policies

Instance administrators can configure onboarding rules under **Settings → Instance → Authentication**:

- **Allow Public Sign-Up**: Turn this off to make your instance invite-only. New users must receive an invitation from an administrator. An instance that accepts sign-ups needs a mail sender.
- **Require Email Verification**: Turn this on to require users to verify their email address before accessing their account.

:::note Enterprise Single Sign-On
Octarq Pro provides Enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) using SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect (OIDC) with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Keycloak, and Google Workspace, along with automated user provisioning.
:::
